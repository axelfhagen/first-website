# Beijing Solo
*August 2025*

This was my first solo trip in Asia. It was a 5-day trip before my exchange in Japan. I could extend my layover from Oslo to Tokyo as long as I wished with no extra cost. Seeing the Great Wall of China had always been a dream of mine, so it was an easy choice.

## Highlights
- Great Wall of China (ofc)
- The Summer Palace (was not overrated at all)
- Beijing nightlife. Incredibily vibrant energy.

I would like to mention I really hated the food as it was hard to find vegetarian food with no google maps and all alternatives being in Mandarin.
